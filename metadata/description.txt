KOTOROOM helps people review their photo library a month at a time and find items that may be taking up storage.

• Use “Keep”, “Bin” and “Undo last swipe” during monthly review.
• Use “Roll the die” to pick an unfinished month.
• Explore “Storage cleanup” for “Exact duplicates”, “Screenshots”, “Large videos” and “Screen recordings”.
• Check items in “Review bin” before keeping them or deleting them from Photos.
• Use “Protected albums” to keep their photos out of review and the bin.
• Keep photos and Live Photos in “Secret Folder” with a PIN and optional Face ID.
• Use “Password generator”, “Email name ideas”, “Check a password” and “Check an email”.
• Continue with “Quiet reminders”, the “Monthly contact sheet” widget and a companion watch view.

“Secret Folder” has no backup. Keep your PIN safe and restore photos before changing devices.
